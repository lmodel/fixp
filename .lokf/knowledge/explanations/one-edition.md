# One edition

FIXP 1.0 is the Technical Standard the FIX Trading Community approved in April 2021. Version 1.1 is a Draft Standard.[^about.md]

Version 1.1's resource files differ from 1.0's only in four field numbers of `MessageTemplate`, and it lacks an Orchestra file. Its chapters add guidance for WebSocket and mend three spellings.[^about.md]

The upstream watch reports a new tag or edition folder.[^about.md]
